# AI Seller Test

## Manual Validation Checklist

### Cold Lead
- Response acknowledges uncertainty/resistance.
- Response proposes low-friction next step.
- No pressure or manipulative urgency.

### Warm Lead
- Response maps pain to concrete mechanism.
- Response keeps one clear CTA.
- Response avoids overloading with multiple offers.

### Hot Lead
- Response gives direct conversion path.
- Response is concise and action-oriented.
- Response confirms next operational step.

### Objection Handling
- Objection is validated first.
- Barrier is reframed into actionable next step.
- Tone remains respectful and non-defensive.

## Pass Criteria
- All four segments behave consistently with expected tone and CTA discipline.
