# Purpose

The Business Funnel exists to define one canonical commercial progression through the Starway ecosystem.

It explains how a person moves from first attention to paid engagement, deeper value, renewal, and advocacy.

This document is not the company definition and does not replace `docs/foundation/01-company.md`.

This document is not the product catalog and does not replace `docs/foundation/02-products.md`.

The company document defines why Starway exists and the principles that govern it.

The product document defines what business products exist and how they relate.

This funnel document defines how a person progresses through those products as a business journey.

# Funnel Principles

1. One canonical entry path.
   The funnel must define one primary business progression that every future document references.

2. Deterministic progression.
   A person should move from one stage to the next through explicit business conditions, not arbitrary interpretation.

3. Value before sale.
   Every step must create clarity, trust, or movement before asking for deeper commitment.

4. One primary next action.
   Each stage must make the next business action obvious.

5. Measurable conversions.
   Every stage must have a business objective and a conversion definition.

6. Business-driven decisions.
   Funnel stages exist to serve commercial and customer outcomes, not implementation convenience.

7. No stage skipping without business justification.
   A later-stage offer must not bypass the value or readiness created by an earlier stage unless a defined premium entry path exists.

8. Recovery is part of the funnel.
   Drop-off and re-entry are expected business realities and must have defined return paths.

The canonical Starway business funnel is:

## Instagram

- Purpose: Create first awareness and resonance.
- Business objective: Generate qualified attention from the right audience.
- Customer objective: Recognize that their problem is real, relevant, and understood.
- Entry conditions: The person encounters Starway through content, referral, or recommendation.
- Success criteria: The person becomes interested enough to continue into a direct relationship channel.
- Primary CTA: Move into Telegram.
- Exit conditions: The person either ignores the message, saves it for later, or enters Telegram.

## Telegram

- Purpose: Convert attention into an active relationship.
- Business objective: Turn passive audience attention into direct engagement.
- Customer objective: Explore the next step in a low-friction environment.
- Entry conditions: The person chooses to continue from Instagram or an equivalent direct contact path.
- Success criteria: The person begins guided interaction and is ready for the Entry Test.
- Primary CTA: Start the Entry Test.
- Exit conditions: The person starts the Entry Test, stays inactive, or exits the conversation.

## Entry Test

- Purpose: Diagnose the person’s current state and establish a meaningful first decision point.
- Business objective: Convert engagement into the first structured commitment.
- Customer objective: Understand what is blocking progress and receive clear language for it.
- Entry conditions: The person enters the guided diagnostic path.
- Success criteria: The person completes the test and receives a usable result.
- Primary CTA: See the result and accept the recommendation.
- Exit conditions: The person completes the test, abandons it, or postpones continuation.

## Entry Recommendation

- Purpose: Translate diagnosis into the next logical business step.
- Business objective: Route the person into the right product or follow-up path without ambiguity.
- Customer objective: Understand what to do next based on their current reality.
- Entry conditions: The person has completed the Entry Test and received a result.
- Success criteria: The person accepts the recommended next step or enters a valid follow-up path.
- Primary CTA: Join FOCUS Membership.
- Exit conditions: The person joins FOCUS, enters a trust-building/recovery path, or disengages.

## FOCUS Membership

- Purpose: Turn insight into rhythm, practice, and repeated action.
- Business objective: Activate the first paid relationship and create visible momentum.
- Customer objective: Start moving in a supported recurring format.
- Entry conditions: The person has enough readiness to commit to structured weekly action.
- Success criteria: The customer becomes an active paying participant and experiences repeated value.
- Primary CTA: Join and participate consistently.
- Exit conditions: The customer renews, upgrades, pauses, expires, or drops off.

## ABSystem Platform

- Purpose: Turn recurring practice into continuity and personal systemization.
- Business objective: Extend retention, deepen value, and increase recurring customer lifetime value.
- Customer objective: Maintain movement between live touchpoints and build a personal system.
- Entry conditions: The customer is ready for continuity beyond the core live-practice layer.
- Success criteria: The customer uses the platform as an ongoing support layer and sees continuity value.
- Primary CTA: Continue consistently inside the platform.
- Exit conditions: The customer renews, upgrades, pauses, expires, or moves toward premium services.

## Premium Services

- Purpose: Provide high-context human support for customers who need deeper transformation.
- Business objective: Deliver premium value and create the high-touch revenue layer.
- Customer objective: Receive more direct, personalized support than scalable products alone can provide.
- Entry conditions: The customer reaches a level of need, readiness, fit, and trust that justifies premium support.
- Success criteria: The customer enters the right premium service with clear fit and intention.
- Primary CTA: Enter the appropriate premium service.
- Exit conditions: The customer continues the service, completes it, renews it, or steps back into lower layers.

## Renewal

- Purpose: Preserve continuity after the initial paid term ends or approaches expiry.
- Business objective: Retain revenue and keep the customer in motion without unnecessary restart friction.
- Customer objective: Continue progress without losing context, rhythm, or access.
- Entry conditions: A paid product is approaching expiration or the customer reaches a continuation decision point.
- Success criteria: The customer renews the correct product or transitions into the correct lower or higher layer.
- Primary CTA: Renew or continue in the right product.
- Exit conditions: The customer renews, changes product level, pauses, or exits the active commercial relationship.

## Advocate

- Purpose: Turn realized value into trust, loyalty, and recommendation.
- Business objective: Increase referrals, reputation, retention depth, and long-term customer goodwill.
- Customer objective: Share, recommend, or stay connected from a place of genuine value.
- Entry conditions: The customer has received meaningful value and has a stable positive relationship with the ecosystem.
- Success criteria: The customer recommends, refers, returns, or remains positively connected to the brand.
- Primary CTA: Refer, share, or remain engaged as a supporter of the ecosystem.
- Exit conditions: The customer becomes inactive or re-enters the funnel later through a new need.

# Funnel Transitions

## Instagram → Telegram

- Source stage: Instagram
- Destination stage: Telegram
- Trigger: The person chooses direct interaction.
- Business validation: Enough resonance exists for the person to continue voluntarily.
- Failure path: The person consumes content but does not engage further.
- Recovery path: Ongoing content, remarketing, referral, or later direct entry.

## Telegram → Entry Test

- Source stage: Telegram
- Destination stage: Entry Test
- Trigger: The person accepts the guided first action.
- Business validation: The person is willing to engage with a structured diagnostic.
- Failure path: Passive reading, hesitation, or drop-off.
- Recovery path: Re-invitation, follow-up messaging, or later re-entry into the same stage.

## Entry Test → Entry Recommendation

- Source stage: Entry Test
- Destination stage: Entry Recommendation
- Trigger: The person completes the diagnostic.
- Business validation: The business now has enough context to recommend a next step.
- Failure path: Test abandonment or incomplete engagement.
- Recovery path: Resume the test, restart later, or re-enter through Telegram.

## Entry Recommendation → FOCUS Membership

- Source stage: Entry Recommendation
- Destination stage: FOCUS Membership
- Trigger: The person accepts the recommended first paid next step.
- Business validation: Readiness is sufficient for structured recurring support.
- Failure path: The person hesitates, declines, or postpones payment.
- Recovery path: Trust-building follow-up, further clarification, or later re-offer.

## FOCUS Membership → ABSystem Platform

- Source stage: FOCUS Membership
- Destination stage: ABSystem Platform
- Trigger: The customer is ready for continuity and deeper personal systemization.
- Business validation: The customer has enough commitment and value recognition to benefit from the platform layer.
- Failure path: The customer remains only in FOCUS, pauses, or disengages.
- Recovery path: Re-introduction after more practice, renewal, or continuity messaging.

## ABSystem Platform → Premium Services

- Source stage: ABSystem Platform
- Destination stage: Premium Services
- Trigger: The customer needs or qualifies for a deeper human-led support layer.
- Business validation: Fit, readiness, trust, and business value are all present.
- Failure path: The customer is not ready, not qualified, or prefers to remain in scalable products.
- Recovery path: Continue in ABSystem Platform or return to the appropriate active layer.

## Premium Services → Renewal

- Source stage: Premium Services
- Destination stage: Renewal
- Trigger: The current premium commitment approaches a decision point.
- Business validation: There is a real continuation decision to make.
- Failure path: The customer completes the service and exits without continuation.
- Recovery path: Offer the right continuation layer, strategic follow-up, or later re-entry.

## FOCUS Membership → Renewal

- Source stage: FOCUS Membership
- Destination stage: Renewal
- Trigger: The membership term approaches expiration.
- Business validation: The customer has active or recent value that justifies a continuation decision.
- Failure path: Non-renewal or silent drop-off.
- Recovery path: Reactivation or return to the correct earlier or adjacent stage.

## ABSystem Platform → Renewal

- Source stage: ABSystem Platform
- Destination stage: Renewal
- Trigger: The platform term approaches expiration.
- Business validation: The customer has enough continuity value to justify a renewal decision.
- Failure path: Non-renewal, churn, or pause.
- Recovery path: Re-activation, downgrade path, or later return.

## Renewal → Advocate

- Source stage: Renewal
- Destination stage: Advocate
- Trigger: The customer continues, receives value, and develops a stable positive relationship with the ecosystem.
- Business validation: Value has become durable enough to support trust and recommendation.
- Failure path: The customer renews without deeper engagement or leaves the ecosystem.
- Recovery path: Continued value delivery, trust-building, and future referral or loyalty opportunities.

# Entry & Exit Rules

## Allowed Entry Points

- Instagram
- Telegram
- Direct referral into Telegram or the Entry Test
- Premium direct entry only where the business intentionally allows it

## Allowed Exit Points

- Exit before Telegram
- Exit during Telegram engagement
- Exit during the Entry Test
- Non-conversion after Entry Recommendation
- Non-renewal after any paid stage
- Completion without continuation after Premium Services

## Re-entry Rules

- A person may re-enter through Telegram after any non-terminal drop-off.
- A former customer may re-enter through the most appropriate current commercial stage rather than repeating every prior step.
- Premium re-entry is allowed only when it remains business-valid and contextually justified.

## Drop-off Scenarios

- Interest without direct engagement
- Telegram engagement without test start
- Test abandonment
- Recommendation without purchase
- Paid entry without continuity
- Non-renewal
- Premium completion without continued relationship

## Recovery Scenarios

- Re-introduction through content or direct messaging
- Re-start from Telegram
- Resume or retake the Entry Test
- Return to the recommended first paid product
- Renewal at the correct layer
- Downgrade to a lower but still relevant product instead of full exit

# Cross-Product Movement

Customers move through products according to the relationships defined in `docs/foundation/02-products.md`.

## Canonical Cross-Product Rules

- Entry Test may lead into FOCUS Membership as the primary first paid step.
- FOCUS Membership may lead into ABSystem Platform when continuity and systemization are the right next value layer.
- ABSystem Platform may lead into Premium Services when the customer needs higher-context human support.
- Premium Services may return the customer into Renewal or an adjacent lower layer depending on fit, duration, and future need.
- A customer may remain in a lower product if that product still matches the customer’s current stage better than the next one.

This document governs movement between product layers.

It does not redefine the products themselves.

# Governance

## Ownership

The business funnel is owned by the business architecture layer of the company.

Changes must be approved by the business owner of the funnel and aligned with the canonical product ecosystem in `docs/foundation/02-products.md`.

## Versioning

This document must evolve as one canonical file.

The current valid funnel is always the latest approved version of this document.

Historical funnel models must be archived rather than left as parallel active definitions.

## Change Rules

- No new funnel stage may be introduced without a business reason.
- No stage may be removed without redefining the transition logic around it.
- No lower-level document may invent a competing funnel.
- Product documents may clarify value and ownership, but this funnel defines progression.

## Backward Compatibility

When the funnel changes:

- existing downstream documents must be aligned to the new funnel;
- historical interpretations must be archived if still needed for traceability;
- no active competing funnel may remain in the repository.

All future lifecycle, AI, operations, and automation documents must reference this funnel instead of redefining customer progression.
